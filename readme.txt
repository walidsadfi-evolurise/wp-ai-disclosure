Tells visitors when they interact with an AI system (EU AI Act, art. 50). Auto-detects chat widgets, adds a block and a shortcode, counts impressions.

== Description ==

Article 50 of the EU AI Act requires that people are told when they interact with an AI system. This plugin shows a short, accessible notice next to chat widgets and AI-assisted forms, automatically on the pages where a known widget is loaded, and keeps a daily impression count (no personal data) for your records.

* Detects Intercom, Crisp, Tidio, Drift, HubSpot, Zendesk, tawk.to, Freshchat, LiveChat, Brevo, Chatbase, Voiceflow, Botpress, Dialogflow Messenger, WPBot, AI Engine and Kognetiks.
* Your own CSS selectors.
* Banner (top/bottom) or inline placement, dismissible per session.
* Block and `[ai_disclosure]` shortcode.
* Default text in EN, FR, DE, ES, IT, NL.
* CSV export of daily impressions.

This plugin is not legal advice.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate it.
3. Go to Settings → AI Disclosure.
